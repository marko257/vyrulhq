Privacy Policy

Vyrul HQ ("we", "us", "our") operates vyrulhq.com and provides managed short-form content services. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

The short version: We collect only what we need to run your account and deliver our service. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties except where required to deliver the service (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processing).

Information We Collect

  • Contact information — name, email address, and phone number you provide when booking a call or signing up.
  • Business information — your brand name, social media handles, and content goals shared during onboarding.
  • Footage and content — raw video files you upload for clipping; these are stored securely and used solely to produce your deliverables.
  • Usage data — standard web analytics (page views, referral source, device type) collected via Vercel Analytics. No personally identifiable information is included.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store card numbers or bank details.

How We Use It

  • To deliver the service — edit clips, post content, and manage your accounts per our agreement.
  • To communicate with you — updates, reports, invoices, and support.
  • To improve service quality — reviewing performance data to inform strategy.
  • To comply with legal obligations — if required by law or legal process.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you cancel, we delete your footage and personal data within 60 days upon written request. Billing records are retained for 7 years as required by law.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@vyrulhq.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies — session cookies required for functionality only. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

Third-Party Services

  • Vercel — hosting and analytics
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Supabase — data storage (client portal)
  • TikTok, Instagram, YouTube — platforms we post to on your behalf

Each third party has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices: encrypted data in transit (HTTPS), encrypted storage, access controls, and regular backups. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the revised version here with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.
